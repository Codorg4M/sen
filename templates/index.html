How RideShare Works

A straightforward 4-step workflow connecting college commuters and city travelers.

1

Search & Select

Riders search available rides by pickup location, destination, travel date, and passenger count.

2

Request Booking

Select seats requested with instant fare estimation (Base ₹30 + ₹12/km). Request status initializes as 'Pending'.

3

Driver Decision

Driver reviews the request, verifies capacity, and accepts or rejects. Available seats update automatically.

4

Ride & Complete

Once journey concludes, driver marks the ride completed. The trip is securely preserved in both users' ride history.
